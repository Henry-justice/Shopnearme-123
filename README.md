### What is ShopNearMe?

ShopNearMe is a free global business directory.

Many small business owners have great products, but customers cannot find them. Customers are searching every day for:

- A plumber near them
- A cake vendor near them
- A tailor near them
- A photographer near them
- A shop that delivers to them

But they end up finding only big companies.

ShopNearMe solves that problem.

We help customers find ANY business near them, and we help small business owners get customers — for FREE.

### How It Works

1.  **Customer:** Choose your country & city, search what you need.
2.  **Customer:** See businesses near you, click WhatsApp to chat directly.
3.  **Business Owner:** Click "Add Your Business", fill your details in 2 minutes, and start getting customers.
